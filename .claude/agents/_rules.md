# Rules shared by the RidePlan analysis agents

- Read `docs/PROJECT.md`, `README.md` and the open issues (`gh issue list -R NanuNic9379/RidePlan`, `gh issue view N`).
- **Analysis only**: do not modify files, do not commit, do not comment on issues, do not create anything on GitHub.
- Reason from your experience in the saddle: what you really need, what drives you mad in the apps you use today
  (Calimoto, Kurviger, Google Maps, Garmin, Komoot, Scenic, MyRoute-app, REVER, Wheelo…). You can search the web
  for real user reviews and complaints to confirm.
- Respect the project principles: no accounts, no servers of our own (c2c sharing), offline first,
  GPX, no data collection, open source GPL-3.0.
- Final report in Italian, concise, in this order:
  1. **Who you are** (one line).
  2. **For issues #1–#7**: what must be there for you (concrete acceptance criteria), what to avoid.
  3. **Gaps**: features missing from the plan that are decisive for you (at most 5, in order of importance),
     each with "why, on the road".
  4. **Priorities**: the 3 things to do first.
  5. **Risks**: what would make you uninstall the app.
